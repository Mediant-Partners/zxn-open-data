# zxn.uk open data

The figures behind zxn.uk's studies of UK small-business websites, as plain CSV files you can check, reuse and build on.

Each study has its own folder under `studies/<id>/` with:

- the aggregate CSV files the published charts are drawn from
- `DICTIONARY.md`: what every column means, with units
- `METHOD.md`: who we checked and how, the sample size and date, what was left out, and the limits

## What is here, and what is not

These files hold totals and shares only. No business, website or person is named, and no row can be traced back to one. Any cell
built from fewer than 10 businesses is left out. We collect passively: one polite visit to public pages, following robots.txt and
honouring every opt-out.

We build and look after websites for small businesses, so we have an interest in this subject. The method notes say where that might
matter.

## Using these figures

You are welcome to use, share and adapt these figures under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence. Please credit "zxn.uk" and link the study
page on [zxn.uk](https://zxn.uk/blog/), so readers can see how the figures were collected.

If you spot a mistake, please open an issue. We will fix it and note the change in the study's `METHOD.md`.
